Transparency & Governance

Ethical Non-Profit Stewardship & Verified Certifications

Registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, InAmigos Foundation maintains rigorous statutory compliance, transparent financial auditing, and government-recognized certifications.

Grassroots Compassion Backed by Total Accountability

InAmigos Foundation is a pan-India Section 8 Non-Profit organization rooted in the core conviction that genuine social progress begins at the grassroots. Headquartered in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, our youth-driven movement bridges community compassion with structured, measurable field operations.

We eliminate bureaucratic friction and intermediary overhead to ensure that every rupee donated reaches hungry families, eager young learners, and distressed stray animals. Our audited accounts and direct handoffs guarantee complete operational transparency.

Core Initiatives

Four Pillars of Ground-Level Social Action

Focused, transparent interventions addressing acute hunger, foundational education, stray animal distress, and women empowerment.

Project Seva

Food distribution, winter relief drives, blanket distribution, and essential nutrition aid for underprivileged families across 28 states.

  • 50,000+ cooked meals & ration kits distributed
  • Annual winter blanket & warm clothing drives
  • Direct emergency relief for flood-hit families
Support Project Seva

Project Bachpanshala

Quality education, free learning resources, stationery kits, and mentorship for street children and first-generation learners.

  • Community open-air learning hubs & evening classes
  • Free textbooks, notebooks & stationery kits
  • 1-on-1 mentorship preventing dropout rates
Support Bachpanshala

Project Jeev

Stray animal rescue, emergency medical treatment, anti-rabies vaccination, and daily feeding programs for voiceless strays.

  • 800+ injured stray animals rescued and treated
  • Daily community feeding for 500+ stray dogs
  • Reflective safety collar drives preventing accidents
Support Project Jeev

Project Udaan

Women empowerment through vocational training, stitching/handicraft skill-building, and menstrual hygiene awareness workshops.

  • Tailoring & stitching vocational workshop cohorts
  • Free sanitary pad distribution & health counseling
  • Micro-entrepreneurship & market linkage support
Support Project Udaan
